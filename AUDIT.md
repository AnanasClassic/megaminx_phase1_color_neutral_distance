# Computational audit

## Scope

This audit covers the new statement

```text
The worst-case color-neutral distance of the first corner-block phase is exactly 9.
```

It does not independently reproduce the public 114-move global bound.

## Checks completed

1. **Coordinate rank/unrank**
   - `encode(decode(i)) == i` for every one of 11,692,800 indices.
   - All decoded edge positions are distinct.

2. **Move implementation**
   - All 12 face generators have order five on sampled coordinates.
   - Every target corner has exactly three incident target edges.
   - Every target edge belongs to exactly two target blocks.

3. **Complete BFS**
   - All 20 target tables were regenerated from source.
   - Every BFS visited exactly 11,692,800 coordinates.
   - The solved coordinate in every table had eccentricity 10, with 159 states at distance 10 and 364,616 states in depths 9--10.
   - All 40 regenerated files were byte-identical to the distributed certificates.

4. **Ordinary coordinate-graph diameter**
   - Orientation translations and tracked-edge relabelling reduce every coordinate to one of 81,200 positional roots.
   - The 120 spatial symmetries reduce these roots to 708 orbit representatives.
   - Complete BFS from all representatives gives 637 representative orbits of eccentricity 10 and 71 of eccentricity 11.
   - Therefore the ordinary graph diameter is 11. The orbit counts are not vertex counts.

5. **Upper-bound CSP**
   - Python solver: 145 recursive search nodes, exactly five solutions.
   - Independent C++ solver: 1,015 recursive search nodes, exactly five solutions.
   - The two programs returned the same five edge permutations.
   - Every permutation has cycle lengths `2,2,2,3,3,3,3,3,3,3,3` and odd parity.

6. **Lower-bound witness**
   - Corner permutation: even.
   - Edge permutation: even.
   - Corner orientation sum: 0 mod 3.
   - Edge orientation sum: 0 mod 2.
   - All 20 local coordinates occur in the depth-9-or-10 sets and not in the depth-10 sets.
   - Distance vector: twenty copies of 9.
   - A second Python implementation independently verifies the same layer memberships.
   - An independent SymPy Schreier--Sims computation obtains the full invariant-compatible group order and confirms direct membership of the witness.

7. **Independent compiler/runtime checks**
   - GCC builds with `-Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic`.
   - GCC's undefined-behavior sanitizer completed the exhaustive coordinate self-test.

8. **Document checks**
   - pdfLaTeX compilation completed successfully.
   - The PDF was rendered to PNG at 150 dpi and visually inspected page by page.
   - No clipped text, missing glyphs, or overlapping elements were found.

## Logical dependency of the lower witness

Reachability is checked in two ways. First, the witness satisfies the standard
Megaminx invariants: independent even corner and edge permutations, corner
twist sum zero, and edge flip sum zero. Second, `verify_reachability_group.py`
constructs the group generated by the twelve face turns on 120 oriented
cubie-position states, confirms that its order equals the full
invariant-compatible state count, and verifies direct membership of the
witness by Schreier--Sims.

## Logical dependency of the 113 corollary

The artifact proves the new first-phase contribution `10 -> 9`. The numerical
corollary `114 -> 113` depends on the previously announced 114-move result of
Botz, Whitmore, and Rokicki. The manuscript labels this dependency explicitly.
